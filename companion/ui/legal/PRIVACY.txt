RAIDER TOOLKIT COMPANION
PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 10 November 2026 (second version: updates and the map tab)

THE SHORT VERSION
- No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no crash reports.
- Your settings and shopping list stay on your own computer.
- The app downloads Raider Toolkit's public data and item pictures from raidkits.com, and checks GitHub for new versions. Like any website, those hosts see your IP address when that happens.
- The Map tab shows the raidkits.com website inside the app, which works the same as visiting the site in a browser.
- The app never reads anything from ARC Raiders.

WHO IS RESPONSIBLE
Kuro, a private individual in Sweden, makes the app and is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Contact: Discord user kuro_1, or the Raider Toolkit Discord (https://discord.gg/NE3ughZTxn).

WHAT IS SAVED ON YOUR COMPUTER
The app saves your settings (keybinds, overlay layout and sizes, region, chosen map), your shopping list, the quests and keys you follow and a copy of the downloaded data, so it works offline and remembers your choices. This is stored only on your computer, in the app's data folders (%LOCALAPPDATA%\com.raidkits.companion, and the panel's size and place in %APPDATA%\com.raidkits.companion). It is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app and deleting those folders removes it.

WHAT THE APP DOWNLOADS
- https://raidkits.com/bot.json: the public Raider Toolkit data (items, keys, quests, map conditions). Checked when the app starts and about every 30 minutes, downloaded again when it is more than 6 hours old.
- Item pictures from https://raidkits.com/i/ when they are shown.
raidkits.com is hosted on GitHub Pages. When your computer downloads these files, GitHub (a US company) receives your IP address and standard request details, as with any website, and keeps them in its logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
Nothing about you, your list or your settings is sent with these requests.

UPDATES
When the app starts and then every 6 hours, it asks GitHub (https://github.com/KuroXiio/raider-toolkit/releases) whether a newer version exists, and downloads it when you choose to install it. GitHub receives your IP address and standard request details, as above. Nothing about you, your list or your settings is sent. Updates are signed, and the app only installs updates signed with Raider Toolkit's key. You can turn the automatic check off in Settings > Updates; "Check now" then checks only when you press it.

THE MAP TAB
The Map tab shows the interactive map from raidkits.com inside the app. It loads that web page exactly as a browser would: raidkits.com is hosted on GitHub Pages (see above), and the site counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not store your IP address. The site's own privacy policy applies to it: https://raidkits.com/#privacy . The website may save its own settings (such as map filters) in the app's browser storage on your computer. The page is only loaded while you have the Map tab open.

WHAT THE APP DOES NOT DO
- It does not read game memory, game files, logs or your screen, and collects no information about your game, account or matches.
- It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- It sends nothing you type to anyone.

LINKS
Links in the app (raidkits.com, Discord) open in your web browser. Those sites' own privacy policies apply.

MICROSOFT WEBVIEW2
The app draws its windows with Microsoft Edge WebView2, a part of Windows. Microsoft may collect diagnostic data from Windows components according to your Windows privacy settings. That is between you and Microsoft; the app doesn't send Microsoft anything itself.

YOUR RIGHTS
Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data is held about you and have it corrected or deleted, and you can object to its use. In practice the app holds no personal data about you anywhere but your own computer. If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, https://www.imy.se/en/) or to the data protection authority where you live.

CHANGES
If the app starts handling data differently, this policy is updated with the new version and the date at the top changes.
