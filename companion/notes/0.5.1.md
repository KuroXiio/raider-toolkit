Big one. The old corner cards are gone and the overlay has been rebuilt from scratch.

**What's new**
- Ctrl+Shift+Space now opens a bar over the game with Map, Route, List, Quests, Conditions and Notes. Open the ones you want, drag them where you want them.
- Pin a widget and it stays on screen while you play, in the exact spot and size it had in the bar. Clicks go straight through it to the game.
- Plan a route without leaving the game: pick your map, tap where you spawned, press Start. The map follows you stop by stop, greys out what's behind you and keeps later stops hidden.
- F7 is next stop and F6 is back (change them under Settings > Keybinds). F7 also starts a route that's ready.
- Switching map asks for your spawn there and plans the route again for that map.
- Search any item or key on the map. You can add it to your list or route right from the result.
- Notes: free text on several pages, and you can pin the page you have open.
- One click connects the app to raidkits.com, so the list you build on the site is the list in the app and "Send to app" just arrives. Pasting a sync code or link still works.
- The main window is now just Home and Settings. Everything else moved into the bar.

**Fixed**
- Pinned widgets never showed while playing with "Only while you play" on, because the game's window wasn't recognised. This was broken in 0.4 as well.
- Pinned widgets blinking twice a second, or disappearing after the bar closed.
- The next/previous stop keys never worked in game.

**Good to know**
- Play in Borderless or Windowed mode.
- The map has its own see-through setting now and is more solid than the other widgets by default.
- Settings > In game has a "Show for 10 seconds" button and a line that tells you which window the app last saw, in case pinned widgets don't show up for you.
- The first start asks once if you want to connect to raidkits.com. You can skip it.

**Installing**
The easy way is the Microsoft Store: https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9NVWVPNN94NF
The installer here isn't code-signed, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Press More info, then Run anyway. If you already have the GitHub version it updates itself.

The app is an ordinary window on top of the game. It doesn't read, change or connect to ARC Raiders. Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Embark Studios, use at your own risk.

The `_Store.msix` file is the package for the Microsoft Store, you don't need it.
