Guide

How does it work?

Plan your raids in a few clicks. No account needed: everything saves in your browser.

  1. 01

    Find your keys

    Open Keys to see what every key opens and how to get it. Press Show on map to jump straight to its door.

  2. 02

    Know where loot spawns

    Search any item in the Item finder for its value, where it spawns and what it recycles into. Map intel shows what each area holds.

  3. 03

    Plan your upgrades

    Set your stations in the Workshop planner, or pick a gun, tier and attachments in the Weapon calculator. Every material you need is added up for you. Missing a blueprint? The Blueprint tracker tells you whether to research it or where to find it.

  4. 04

    Link ARCTracker (optional)

    Already use ARCTracker? Link it once and the site keeps itself up to date: your stash fills in the Have counts in the shopping list and Workshop materials, your station levels fill in the Workshop planner, the Weapon calculator knows which tiers you already have, and the Blueprint tracker ticks off every blueprint you have learned. It syncs every hour by itself. ARCTracker gets your game data from its ARC Tracker Link app, so install that first.

  5. 05

    Build your shopping list

    Press + Shopping list anywhere. Your list combines everything, tracks what you already have and exports to Discord, Steam or plain text.

FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

Do I need an account? Where is my stuff saved?

No account needed. Your plans, shopping list and settings are saved in this browser on this device. Clearing your browser data or switching to another device starts you fresh.

How do I share a weapon build or move it to another device?

In the Weapon calculator, press Copy build code and paste it anywhere, for example in Discord. Anyone can open it with Load a build.

What does the number on a map pin mean?

Several keys open doors in the same building. Hover or tap the pin to see all of them and which door each one opens.

Why does one key have several pins?

Some keys, like the Blue Gate Cellar Key, open more than one door. Each pin is one of them, so if the first door is already looted, try the next.

Is the pin on the exact door?

Hover the pin to check. It says whether it marks the door itself or just the building or area.

How is DPS calculated?

Damage × fire rate. It is meant for comparing weapons with each other, and does not count reloads, headshots or shields.

Why do some weapon stats show “—” or a note about sources?

Some numbers are missing from the community data set. Where we could, gaps are filled from arcraiders.wiki or arcraidershub.com and marked under the table. “—” means no source has that number yet.

Why do the weapon stats only open at the end?

The Weapon calculator goes step by step. The stats open once every attachment slot is set; choose None to leave a slot empty, or press Skip to stats.

How does the shopping list count items?

Everything you add becomes its own group. If two groups need the same item, the Have number is shared between them, so nothing gets counted twice. Tick an item once you have all of it.

What do the export options do?

Combine everything merges all groups into one total list. Include items I've already got keeps finished items in the export, marked as done. Then pick Plain, Discord or Steam formatting and copy.

How accurate are the map conditions?

They are estimated from a rotation we recorded hour by hour. Most events repeat every 25 hours, so the timers are usually right, but roughly one hour in ten can differ. Pick your server region on the map card, and check the in-game map screen before you count on an event.

How up to date is the data?

The badge at the bottom of every page shows the game patch and when the data was last updated. The site refreshes its data automatically every day.

Why does a map say WIP?

Something for that map is still missing, like the map image or key pins. The map card says exactly what.

How do I link my ARCTracker account?

Press Link ARCTracker at the top of the page. In ARCTracker, open Settings → Developer Access, create a personal key with profile, stash, hideout, blueprints and quests read access, and paste it in. Your raider name and level then show in the top corner. From then on, your stash fills in the Have counts in the shopping list and Workshop materials, your station levels fill in the Workshop planner, the Weapon calculator answers “Do you own it?” for you, and the Blueprint tracker ticks off what you have learned. If you have not learned a blueprint yet, its materials are left out and the site shows where to find it, including quests you have not done that give it. You can still change anything by hand.

How often does ARCTracker sync, and is my key safe?

It syncs by itself once an hour while the site is open, and you can press Refresh up to 2 more times per hour (a countdown shows when the next one is available). Your key is saved only in this browser and only sent to ARCTracker. Unlink deletes it; your Have counts stay.

ARCTracker is linked but nothing fills in?

If the dot turns orange, your key works but ARCTracker has no game data for your account yet. ARCTracker gets your stash, stations, blueprints and quests from its own PC app, ARC Tracker Link. Install it from ARCTracker, link your ARC Raiders account there, press Sync now, then press Refresh here.

If only something is off, like your level or one station, open the ARCTracker box at the top, press Copy sync details and send it to us in Discord. It shows how ARCTracker sends your data, without your key.

How does the Blueprint tracker choose between researching and finding?

If a quest you have not finished gives the blueprint, it suggests the quest first, because quest rewards are guaranteed (you can turn this off). Next comes researching it, if your Research Station is high enough, and otherwise the best place to find it, with the map condition and a live timer for when that condition is next up. The + buttons add it all to your shopping list: research materials, the quest and anything it asks you to hand in, research items to cover the Research Points if you pick one, and the materials to craft each item. Your Research Station level comes from the Workshop planner, or from ARCTracker when linked.

Where do I get Research Points?

Study research items after you extract: Battered Paperback (50), Raider Logbook (100), Exodus Technical Handbook (150), ARC Data Module (200) and Frigate Diagnostic Node (1,000, only on the ARC Frigate). The Blueprint tracker shows how many you still need and when the next Frigate is up.

Is this official?

No. This is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Embark Studios.

I found a mistake or have an idea. Where do I tell you?

Drop it in our Discord. Corrections are added for everyone.

Data sources

Notes per tool

Workshop. Levels 1 to 3 of the six Workshop benches come from the game data (v1.42). Gunsmith IV, the Outpost and the Research Station come from October 2026 guides. Research node costs, and what is unlocked at each level, are only partly published. Stash counts are saved in this browser only.

Weapons. The data set lists the materials for each tier, not the Gunsmith level each upgrade needs, so that is only shown for crafting the weapon and the attachments. Amplification Module counts are not published, so they are shown as unknown, never guessed. Weapon and attachment costs, tier bonuses and repair costs are from the game data (v1.42); a dash in the tier table means the data set has no value for that tier. Blueprint locations are from community guides. Kinetic Converter, Horizontal Grip and Silencer III cannot be crafted: they are found as loot, and the game's quest and project data name a guaranteed source where there is one. Corrections from the 2.0 and 2.3 patch notes are applied: the Anvil is Rare and lost its tech-mod slot (the Anvil Splitter is now part of the Amplified Anvil), Tian Wen no longer sells the Anvil or Hullcracker, creds were removed so cred prices are gone, and six legendary blueprints can drop in any major map condition. The Bantam and Stiletto (new in 2.0) are listed as WIP until their recipes are published. Build codes contain only the weapon, tiers, amplification and attachments.

Maps. Map images are the in-game maps from the data set. Named areas, ziplines and per-map conditions come from community map pages; the full condition list is from the game data. Pendola Pass has no map image or area list in the sources used yet. The interactive-map buttons link to arcraidersmaps.app. Zoomed-in views load the full original images (Buried City 6144 px, Stella Montis 5120 px, Dam 4260 px, the rest about 4000 px). How sharp they look depends on the source: Dam and Spaceport have fine detail but film-grain, Blue Gate is softer, and Riven Tides is the softest; the data set has no sharper versions with area names on them.

Keys. What each key opens comes from the game's item descriptions, with door details, how to get it and what is inside from the community guides listed above. Guides sometimes disagree; where they do, both versions are shown. Map pins mark the named area printed on the game map, not the exact door, and only keys whose area is labelled on the map have a pin. Riven Tides and Pendola Pass keys have no pins: the Riven Tides map image has no area labels and there is no Pendola Pass map image yet.

Blueprints. The blueprint list is every blueprint-locked item in the game data, plus the Bantam, Stiletto, Grappling Hook, Tether Launcher and Yank Grenade from update 2.x. Research Station costs (level, Research Points and materials) are from ARCTracker's research list. Where to find each blueprint is from the community guides listed above; map conditions use the estimated rotation, so the timers can be off. Research Point values are from launch-day guides for Frozen Trail and may change with balance updates. Outpost furniture designs and Amplified weapon perks are not included.

Shopping list. Saved in this browser only. “Have” counts are shared across all groups and taken off the first groups first; the export lists what is still missing. Materials to craft an item come from the game data recipes.

Items. Stats, values, vendors, recycling and recipes are game data. Where-to-find notes come from the community guides listed above. Items added in update 2.x have no value or rarity in the data yet.
