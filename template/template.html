Guide

How does it work?

Plan your raids in a few clicks. No account needed: everything saves in your browser.

  1. 01

    Find any item

    Search the Item finder for value, recycling and where things spawn. Click an item name anywhere on the site for its details, and press Show on map to see only its spots.

  2. 02

    Read the map

    Maps marks extractions, Raider hatches, spawns, containers, plants and key doors. Use Filter to choose what shows. The map card counts down to the next map condition.

  3. 03

    Plan your upgrades

    Set your stations in the Workshop planner, or pick a gun, tier and attachments in the Weapon calculator. Every material you need is added up for you. Missing a blueprint? The Blueprint tracker tells you whether to research it or where to find it.

  4. 04

    Build your shopping list

    Press + Shopping list anywhere. The cart button in the corner opens your list from any page, keeps count of what you have, and Share list makes a link your squad can open.

  5. 05

    Link ARCTracker (optional)

    Link it once and your stash, station levels, learned blueprints and finished quests fill in by themselves, synced every hour. Install the ARC Tracker Link app first.

  6. 06

    Plan your route

    Press Plan a route, tap where you spawned and get a numbered path through the containers and plants you need, spread along the way to the exit.

FAQ

Good to know

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

Getting started
Do I need an account? Where is my stuff saved?

No account needed. Your plans, shopping list and settings are saved in this browser on this device, so clearing your browser data or switching to another device starts you fresh. More in the privacy policy.

How up to date is the data?

The badge at the bottom of every page shows the game patch and when the data was last updated. The site rebuilds itself every day from its sources, which are listed on the Info page.

Is this official?

No. Raider Toolkit is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Embark Studios.

Maps and routes
How do I choose what the map shows?

Open the Filter tab beside the map (under it on phones) and tick what you want: extractions, Raider hatches, player spawns, supply stations, locked rooms, ziplines, containers, ARC, plants, events, quest spots and more. Open a group with its arrow to switch single types on or off, like only Raider caches. Your choice is remembered. Hover a marker on a computer, or tap it on a phone, to see what it is.

How do I see where an item spawns?

Press Show on map on an item, or click an item with a pin count in Loot on this map under the map. The map then shows only that item's spots, with buttons for the other maps it is on; press × to get your own filter back.

Spots are the plants it grows on, the ARC that drop it, the Frigate or Emperor for boss loot, and containers tagged with the item's loot area. Not every container is tagged yet, so treat the spots as a place to start looking, not a full list.

How does the route planner work?

Open the Plan a route tab beside the map, or press Plan a route on your shopping list. Items you still need that can be found on that map are ticked for you, and you can add more. Then tap where you spawned; only spawn points show while you pick.

The planner picks the exit with the most of your items on the way (or the one you choose) and spreads stops along the path, zig-zagging between the containers and plants you need. Spots in the same building become one stop. Items that only come from ARC, like ARC Alloy, are not on the route: they are under ARC drops, which shows where those ARC are found. The slider sets how many stops, up to 80. Lines are straight, not walking paths, and containers are random, so treat it as a plan, not a promise.

How accurate are the map conditions?

They are worked out from a recorded rotation that repeats every 25 hours. The timers are usually right but can be off now and then, and a patch can change the rotation. Pick your server region on the map card, and check the in-game map screen before you count on an event.

Can the map show me where a quest goes?

Yes. In the Quest tracker, quests with known spots have a Show location or Show route button. A route numbers each step and draws a dashed line between them, so quests where you pick something up and bring it somewhere show both ends. Steps with no known spot are greyed out.

What do the key pins mean?

A number on a pin means several keys open doors there; hover or tap it to see each key and its door. Some keys, like the Residential Master Key, open more than one door and get a pin for each, so if one room is looted, try the next. The pin also says whether it marks the door itself or only the area.

Weapons and blueprints
How do I share a weapon build or move it to another device?

In the Weapon calculator, press Copy build code and paste it anywhere, for example in Discord. Anyone can open it with Load a build.

Why do the weapon stats only open at the end?

The Weapon calculator goes step by step. The stats open once every attachment slot is set; choose None to leave a slot empty, or press Skip to stats.

How is DPS calculated?

Damage × fire rate. It is for comparing weapons with each other, and ignores reloads, headshots and shields.

Why do some weapon stats show “—”?

Some numbers are missing from the game data. Where we could, gaps are filled from arcraiders.wiki or arcraidershub.com and marked under the table. “—” means no source has that number yet.

How does the Blueprint tracker choose between researching and finding?

If a quest you have not finished gives the blueprint, it suggests the quest first, because quest rewards are guaranteed (untick Use a quest when one gives the blueprint to skip this). Next comes research, if your Research Station is high enough, and otherwise the best place to find it, with the map condition and a timer for when it is next up.

The + buttons put it all on your shopping list: research materials, the quest and its hand-ins, research items to cover the Research Points if you pick one, and the materials to craft each item. Your Research Station level comes from the Workshop planner, or from ARCTracker when linked.

Where do I get Research Points?

Study research items after you extract: Battered Paperback (50), Raider Logbook (100), Exodus Technical Handbook (150), ARC Data Module (200) and Frigate Diagnostic Node (1,000, only on the ARC Frigate). The Blueprint tracker shows how many you still need and when the next Frigate is up.

Shopping list
How does the shopping list count items?

Everything you add becomes its own group. If two groups need the same item, the Have number is shared between them, so nothing is counted twice. Tick an item once you have all of it. The cart button in the corner opens your list from any page, sorted by the loot area where you will find most of it.

How do I share my shopping list?

Press Share list on the Shopping list page or in the cart panel. The link holds the whole list, so nothing is uploaded anywhere. Whoever opens it can add your list to theirs or replace theirs with it. Your Have counts are not shared.

What do the export options do?

Combine everything merges all groups into one total list. Include items I've already got keeps finished items in the export, marked as done. Then pick Plain, Discord or Steam formatting and copy.

ARCTracker and quests
How do I link my ARCTracker account?

ARCTracker reads your game data with its PC app, so first install ARC Tracker Link and connect your ARC Raiders account there. Then press Link ARCTracker at the top of this site. In ARCTracker, open Settings → Developer Access, create a personal key with profile, stash, hideout, blueprints and quests read access, and paste it in.

From then on your stash fills in the Have counts, your station levels fill in the Workshop planner, the Weapon calculator knows what you own, the Blueprint tracker ticks off what you have learned and the Quest tracker shows your quests. If you have not learned a blueprint yet, its materials are left out and the site shows where to find it. You can still change anything by hand.

How often does it sync, and is my key safe?

It syncs once an hour while the site is open, and Refresh gives you up to 2 extra syncs per hour. Your key is saved only in this browser and passes through the site's relay to ARCTracker without being stored. Unlink deletes it; your Have counts stay. More in the privacy policy.

ARCTracker is linked but nothing fills in?

If the dot turns orange, your key works but ARCTracker has no game data for your account yet. Install ARC Tracker Link, link your ARC Raiders account there, press Sync now, then press Refresh here.

If only one thing is off, like your level or one station, open the ARCTracker box at the top, press Copy sync details and send it to us in Discord. It shows how ARCTracker sends your data, without your key.

Why is the Quest tracker greyed out?

It needs to know which quests you have finished, and only ARCTracker can tell the site that. Link ARCTracker (see above) and the Quest tracker fills in by itself. Quests added after the last data update are not listed yet; the tracker tells you how many of your finished quests that is.

Skill tree
How does the skill tree planner work?

Click a skill to add a point, and right-click it (or use − under the tree) to take one back. The diamonds work like in the game: the middle one opens a choice of two skills (it needs 10 points in that branch), and the two at the top share a choice of three, the first at 30 points and the second at 40. The second skill of each path needs a point in the first.

Set how many points you have at the top: one per level, 76 at level 75 and up to 91 with Expedition bonus points. Pick order lists your points in the order you spent them, so you know what to take at each level.

Where do the recommended builds come from?

They are 2.x builds published by Gamer Guides, Power Up Gaming and AOEAH, each linked on its card. Step by step plays a build point by point, the way you would level it: the guide's own order where it gives one (Power Up Gaming's phases, Gamer Guides' first 24 points), otherwise each branch up to its middle choice, then the late skills and end picks. Load puts the build in your planner; your own build comes back with Bring mine back. A guide build that breaks the tree's rules is left out.

What does the heart on a build do?

It likes the build. Everyone sees the total, the builds are sorted by likes, and the most liked one gets a star. One like per build per visitor; press it again to take it back.

How do I share a skill build?

Press Share build to copy a link that holds the whole build, pick order included. Copy as text gives a short summary with the link, ready for Discord. Opening someone's link replaces your build in that browser, and Bring mine back restores yours.

Can it load my skills from ARCTracker?

Not yet. ARCTracker does not share skill trees with other sites, so the planner cannot read what you have spent. When ARCTracker is linked, Use my level sets your points from your raider level.

Feedback
Is there a Discord bot?

Yes. Add the Raider Toolkit bot to your server, or to your own account to use it anywhere. /item, /quest and /key look things up, /mapconditions shows what is on each map now and next, and /build shows the recommended skill builds. To share a weapon build, press Copy build code in the Weapon calculator and paste it into /weapon; for a shopping list, paste its Share list link into /list.

I found a mistake or have an idea. How do I tell you?

Press Report a problem at the bottom of any page, or Report something wrong on the Maps page. Reports go straight to a private channel on our Discord; add your Discord name if you are happy for us to ask you about it. Ideas and questions are welcome in the Discord too. Corrections are added for everyone.

Data sources

Known gaps

Notes per tool

Workshop. Levels 1 to 3 of the six Workshop benches are from the game data. Gunsmith IV, the Outpost and the Research Station are from October 2026 guides. Stash counts are saved in this browser only.

Weapons. Crafting, upgrade and repair costs and tier bonuses are from the game data; a dash in the tier table means there is no value for that tier. The 2.0 and 2.3 patch notes are applied on top: the Anvil is Rare and lost its tech-mod slot, Tian Wen no longer sells the Anvil or Hullcracker, creds are gone, and six legendary blueprints can drop in any major map condition. The Kinetic Converter, Horizontal Grip and Silencer III cannot be crafted; they drop as loot, and a guaranteed source is named where the game has one. Where no guide states an Amplification Module's mark, it is worked out from the weapon's rarity. Build codes contain only the weapon, tiers, amplification and attachments.

Maps. The images are 4096 px top-down renders from the ARC Raiders Wiki, with MetaForge's community markers lined up on them. Named areas, ziplines and per-map conditions come from community map pages, and the full condition list from the game data. Loot on this map groups items by the loot category the game gives them (Residential, Medical and so on), and “Special here” lists items the guides tie to that map or its conditions. Interactive map opens the same map on arcraidersmaps.app.

Keys. What each key opens is from the game's item descriptions; door details, how to get the key and what is inside are from community guides, with both versions shown where guides disagree. Pins mark the door where MetaForge's community map has it, and otherwise the named area.

Blueprints. Every blueprint-locked item in the game data, plus the Bantam, Stiletto, Grappling Hook, Tether Launcher and Yank Grenade from update 2.x. Research costs are from ARCTracker's research list, where to find each blueprint is from community guides, and Research Point values are from October 2026 guides. Outpost furniture designs and Amplified weapon perks are not included.

Quests. The quest list is from the game data, map spots and routes from MetaForge, and which quests you have finished from ARCTracker.

Skill tree. The 2.0 tree (Frozen Trail) is not in the game data set yet, which still has the old tree, so it is kept by hand from the 2.0 patch notes and community guides: which skills there are, their ranks, how they connect, and the 10, 30 and 40 point gates. The values shown at max ranks come from MetaForge's skill builder. ARCTracker does not share skill trees, so the planner cannot load yours.

Items. Stats, vendors, recycling and recipes are from the game data, with current values, rarity and weight from MetaForge. Where-to-find notes are from community guides.

Shopping list. Saved in this browser only. Have counts are shared across all groups and taken off the first groups first; the export lists what is still missing.

Privacy policy

What the site saves, what it sends, and who sees it.

Overview

  • No account, no cookies, no ads, no tracking across sites.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and does not identify you. I only see totals, such as how many visits a day and from which countries.
  • Your plans, stash counts, shopping list and settings are saved in your own browser. Nobody else can see them, not even me.
  • Only three things ever leave your browser through this site, and only when you choose to: a problem report you send, ARCTracker syncs if you link your account, and a like you give a recommended skill build.
  • Fonts, icons and map images are served from this site. The only other things a page fetches are the visit counter script from Cloudflare and the like counts on the skill tree page, from the site's own relay.

Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who runs the site

I'm Kuro, a private person in Sweden, and Raider Toolkit is my hobby project. I decide how the site handles personal data, which makes me the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Questions about this page, or a request about your data: message me at kuro_1 on Discord, or ask in the Raider Toolkit Discord.

Saved in your browser

The tools save your work in your browser's local storage on this device: Workshop and stash counts, the shopping list, tracked items and quests, blueprint and weapon settings, your language and similar choices. If you link ARCTracker, your personal key and the data it syncs are saved there too.

None of this is sent to the site, and the site sets no cookies. It is only there so the tools remember your work. To remove it, press Unlink in the ARCTracker panel, or clear the site data for raidkits.com in your browser settings.

Problem reports

A report sent with Report a problem contains your message, the topic you picked, the Discord name you typed (optional), the page you were on (and the map or search, if any) and the site language. It is passed through the site's relay, a small Cloudflare Worker, and posted to a private channel on the Raider Toolkit Discord.

To stop spam, the relay counts reports per IP address in Cloudflare's cache for 15 minutes, then the count expires. Your IP address is not added to the report and is not stored anywhere else.

Why: to find and fix mistakes on the site (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). How long: reports are kept in Discord until they are no longer needed. Ask and yours is deleted.

ARCTracker linking (optional)

If you link ARCTracker, the site uses your personal key to fetch your stash, Workshop levels, learned blueprints, finished quests, player name and level from ARCTracker. Requests go through the site's relay, which adds the site's own app key, forwards them to ARCTracker and hands the answer straight back. The relay does not save your key or your data.

A sync runs when you link, about once an hour while the site is open, and when you press Refresh. What comes back is saved in your browser only. Unlink removes the key and the synced data from this browser (Have counts it filled in stay until you change them). To cut off access completely, delete the key in your ARCTracker account. ARCTracker's own privacy policy covers what ARCTracker stores.

Why: you asked for it by linking (consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Unlinking withdraws it.

Hosting and other services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser loads the site and keeps it in its logs for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. The relay and the visitor statistics run on Cloudflare, which handles the IP addresses of requests sent to it the same way. Reports are delivered through Discord. All three are US companies, so this data can be processed in the US; each one explains its safeguards in its own privacy policy.

Links to other sites (Discord, MetaForge, ARCTracker, the ARC Raiders Wiki, arcraidersmaps.app) contact those sites only when you click them.

Visitor statistics

To see how many people use the site, each page load is counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics. A small script from Cloudflare reports the page address, the address of the site you came from, your browser and device type, and page load times. Cloudflare works out your country from your IP address, but does not store the IP address, sets no cookies, does not fingerprint your device and does not follow you to other sites. I only see totals, never single visitors. See Cloudflare Web Analytics for details.

Why: to know how much the site is used, where visitors come from and which tools are worth improving (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Blocking the script with an ad or tracker blocker does not affect the site.

Build likes

Liking a recommended build on the skill tree page sends the build's name to the site's relay, which adds one to its count. Only the build and its count are stored, in Cloudflare. To stop the same visitor liking a build twice, the relay remembers your IP address together with that build in Cloudflare's cache for one day; it is not stored with the likes. Which builds you liked is saved in your browser so the heart stays filled.

Why: to show which builds players like (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Discord bot

When you use one of the Raider Toolkit bot's commands in Discord, Discord sends the command, what you typed into it and your Discord user and server IDs to the bot, which runs as a Cloudflare Worker. The bot only uses this to answer you. It saves nothing and keeps no logs; the answer is built from the same public data as this site. Discord's own privacy policy covers what Discord stores.

Share links and build codes

A shared shopping list or weapon build is stored inside the link or code itself. Nothing is uploaded, and anyone you give the link or code to can see what is in it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data the site holds about you, and have it corrected or deleted. You can also object to how it is used, or withdraw consent (for ARCTracker, by unlinking). In practice, the only thing the site can hold about you is a report you sent. Contact me as described above.

If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this page

If the site starts handling data differently, I update this page first and change the date at the top.
